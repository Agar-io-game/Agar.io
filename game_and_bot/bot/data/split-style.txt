Which cell splits: a split walks the unit's cells in creation order (lowest id first) and every cell of 44+
mass splits until the unit has 16. A cell that splits keeps its place; its new half goes to the end. So
with 8 cells or fewer every cell splits at once (each gets a twin thrown the same way: your neat pairs and
rows); with more, only the oldest ones do, often the small cells at the back.
